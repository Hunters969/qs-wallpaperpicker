## ✨ What Is qs-wallpaperpicker?

qs-wallpaperpicker is a standalone wallpaper picker. It was originally built as part of a larger desktop setup (a "rice"), then taken out so it can run on its own — no need to install the rest of that package just to change your wallpaper.

Instead of scrolling a boring list of file names, you get a visual deck of cards. Each card shows a wallpaper painted in the colour scheme it would give your desktop, so you can see straight away how a picture will affect the mood of your whole screen. The cards are fanned out along the bottom edge, and the picker's own colours shift to match whichever card is in front as you browse.

If you have ever spent ten minutes clicking through wallpapers trying to find one that "fits", this is the tool that fixes that.

## 🖱️ How to Use the Picker

The picker is designed to be used with a mouse and a couple of keyboard shortcuts.

- **Browse the deck** — move along the fanned-out cards at the bottom of the screen. The card in front is the one being previewed on the background.
- **Set a wallpaper** — select the card you want and confirm it. The wallpaper is applied and the picker's colours switch to match it.
- **Toggle live preview** — press the play button, or press `V` on your keyboard, to turn the live preview on and off. With it on, animated and video wallpapers play as you browse; with it off, you get a still frame and a lighter, faster deck.
- **Close the picker** — dismiss it when you are done; your wallpaper stays applied.

Everything is immediate. There is no "apply" dialog, no confirmation pop-up, no restart needed.

## 🗂️ Sorting Your Wallpaper Deck

A large wallpaper folder can be overwhelming. The picker gives you three ways to arrange the deck, and each one turns browsing into something closer to flipping through a magazine:

**By name** — alphabetical order. Best if you have your own naming system and know exactly which file you are looking for.

**By colour** — the deck is arranged around the hue wheel, so your wallpapers flow from red through orange, yellow, green, blue, purple and back again. This is the setting that makes the deck feel like a genuine paint-chip fan, and it is the fastest way to find something that matches a mood rather than a filename.

**Newest first** — your most recently added wallpapers appear at the front. Handy if you just downloaded a batch and want to try them out.

Switch between them whenever you like. Your choice affects only the order of the deck, never the files themselves.

The picker handles more than still images:

- **Videos** — mp4, webm, mkv and mov files play as moving wallpapers.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

**My wallpaper folder looks empty in the picker.** Double-check that the picker is pointing at the folder where your wallpapers actually are. If you keep them in a subfolder, point the picker at that subfolder instead.

**The deck feels sluggish with video wallpapers.** Turn off live preview with the play button or the `V` key. Videos will show a still frame instead of playing, which is much lighter.

**A wallpaper sets but the colours look wrong.** Colour schemes are read from the image itself. Very dark, very bright, or low-contrast images can produce palettes that are hard to read — trying a different card usually fixes it.

**I can't find a wallpaper I just downloaded from Wallhaven.** It lands in your wallpaper folder. If your deck is sorted "newest first", it will be at the front.

**The picker doesn't appear.** Make sure the application is actually running, then try closing and reopening it. If it still does not appear, re-download from the link above to get a fresh copy.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to install the rest of the desktop setup it came from?**
No. That is the point of this version. It was pulled out of a larger setup specifically so it can run on its own.

**Will it change or delete my wallpaper files?**
No. It reads them, previews them, and applies the one you choose. Your files stay where they are, untouched.

**Can I use it without internet?**
Yes, for your local wallpapers and favourites. The Wallhaven search is the only part that needs a connection.

**What does "paint-chip card" actually mean?**
Each wallpaper is shown inside a card coloured with the palette it would give your desktop. It is a preview of the theme, not just the picture — so you can judge a wallpaper by how it will make everything else look.

**Does it remember my favourites?**
Yes. Wallpapers you have hearted stay in your favourites view.

**Can I use videos as wallpapers?**
Yes — mp4, webm, mkv and mov are all supported, along with animated GIF and WebP.
